"Which holiday home shall we take?" – HolidayVote turns forty links in the group chat into a decision you make together.

You're planning a holiday with friends or family. Everyone sends links from Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, Novasol and the like – and in the end nobody remembers who liked which house. HolidayVote keeps all the houses in one place, everyone rates them, and the ranking shows at a glance which house the group likes best.

CREATE YOUR TRIP
• Name, destination and dates – done
• Invite friends and family by link or 6-character code
• No sign-up, no e-mail address, no password

COLLECT HOUSES
• Paste a link – from Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, Novasol and many other sites
• Preview of each listing with picture – pictures stay on your phone; only the link with title and description goes to the server
• Enter the price and HolidayVote works out the price per person
• All houses on a map

DECIDE TOGETHER
• Everyone rates every house with 1 to 5 stars
• Each person has one favourite – and one veto for the absolute no-go
• Comments on every house
• The ranking shows the group's top picks
• Booked? Whoever created the trip marks the booked house – everyone knows

ALWAYS UP TO DATE
• Ratings and new houses from the others arrive within seconds
• Works offline too – everything syncs as soon as you're back online
• New phone? With your recovery code you're back in right away
• iPhone, iPad and Android – everyone plans along

FREE AND PRIVATE
• Free, no ads, no tracking
• No account, no e-mail address, no password
• Your trip is visible only to the people you invite – stored on a server in the EU
