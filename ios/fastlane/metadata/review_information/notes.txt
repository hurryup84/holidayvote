1. SCREEN RECORDING
Attached (App Review Information → Attachment), recorded on a physical iPhone with the latest iOS. It starts with launching the app and shows: creating a vacation, adding holiday homes by link (with the live preview), rating, favourite and veto, comments, the ranking, the map, sharing (invite, join with a code), reporting content and a member, blocking a member, and "Delete my data" (account deletion). There is no registration and no login.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
HolidayVote helps friends or a family choose a holiday home together. Problem: everyone sends links to holiday homes (Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo …) into a group chat, and nobody keeps track of who liked which house. The organiser creates a vacation (name, destination, dates) and invites the others with a code or link. Everyone adds links (the app shows a live preview; only the link is stored), rates the houses with 1–5 stars, sets one favourite and one veto, and comments. A ranking shows the group's top picks, with price per person and a map. The organiser marks the house that was booked.

3. HOW TO TEST (no login needed)
Sharing needs at least two people (two devices). With one device, please use our demo vacation:
a) Join the demo vacation: start the app → "I was invited" → enter the code {{INVITE_CODE}} and any name → accept the Terms of Use → "Join" → the vacation "Review trip" opens. It already contains houses, ratings and comments added by the member "Demo".
- Rate a house with stars, set it as your favourite or veto it, write a comment. The ranking updates at once.
- Add a house: "+" → paste the link of any holiday-home listing (e.g. from booking.com or airbnb.com) → the preview appears → save.
- Map: shows the houses of the vacation.
- Report content: open a house → "…" (top right) → "Report content"; for a comment: tap someone else's comment → choose a reason.
- Report / block a member: in the vacation, tab "People" → tap "Demo" → "Report person" / "Block".
- Invite: the person icon at the top right of a vacation (or "People" → "Invite") shows a code and a link to share.
b) Alone: create your own vacation (name, destination, dates) and add houses – everything works the same; the invite code can be entered on a second device.
c) Account deletion: Settings → "Delete my data" → confirm. This deletes the anonymous account and its server data.
Content is only visible to people invited to the same vacation: no public content, no discovery, no contact with strangers. Terms of Use (zero tolerance): https://holidayvote.support-moidev.workers.dev/nutzungsbedingungen. Reports reach the developer and are reviewed within 24 hours; content is removed and accounts banned when needed.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Supabase (EU): database, anonymous accounts and realtime updates for shared vacations.
- Cloudflare: our website (privacy policy, support, terms, invite links) and a bot check (Cloudflare Turnstile) before an anonymous account is created.
- OpenStreetMap: map tiles and address search (Nominatim) for the map.
- Link previews: the app loads the public page of the holiday-home link the user added, directly from the device, to show its title and picture; nothing of it is stored except the link.
No in-app purchases, no AI services, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Listing links open the provider's website in the browser.

5. REGIONS
The app works the same in all regions. The interface is in English and German and follows the device language.

6. REGULATION / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable: no regulated industry, no booking or payment in the app, no licensed third-party content. Links are added by the users; listing pictures are only shown as a preview of the linked page and cached on the user's device; they are never uploaded to our server.
